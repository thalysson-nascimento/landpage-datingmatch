🔒 Privacy & Account Management

Delete Your DatingMatch Account

Learn how to permanently remove your DatingMatch account and associated personal data. We believe users should always have full control over their information and privacy.

How to Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your DatingMatch - Chat & Friends account directly inside the app.

1
Open the DatingMatch app

Log in to your account using your registered credentials.

2
Go to your profile settings

Tap your profile menu located and open the Profile section.

3
Select “Delete Account”

Confirm the deletion request. Depending on your security settings, you may be asked to re-enter your credentials.

4
Account deletion completed

Once confirmed, your account will be permanently deleted and you will be redirected to the login screen.

What Happens to Your Data?

When you request account deletion, we apply the following privacy and data retention policies.

✅ Data Deleted Immediately

  • Name, email address and profile information
  • Photos uploaded to your profile
  • Messages, likes and app interactions
  • Preferences and account settings
  • Match history and related activity

⏳ Data Retained Temporarily

  • Security and fraud prevention logs
  • Reports involving abusive or harmful behavior
  • Payment records processed by Stripe
  • Technical logs required for legal compliance

Payment Information

If you purchased subscriptions or premium features, payment-related information may be securely retained by Stripe, our payment processor, in accordance with financial, tax and regulatory obligations.

DatingMatch does not store complete payment card information on its own servers.

Security & Fraud Prevention

Certain security-related logs may be temporarily retained to help prevent fraud, unauthorized access, abuse, scams and harmful activity on the platform.

These records are automatically deleted after a limited retention period unless longer storage is legally required.
